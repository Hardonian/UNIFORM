## Published example

The four matrices in Solan–Vieille Figure 2 were transcribed into all 15 mask payoffs.
The exact one-shot checker finds no pure instant equilibrium. Under the paper's
period-two symmetry `(x,0,z,0)`, `(0,x,0,z)`, active-player indifference yields

```
2*x*z^2 - 5*x*z + 3*x + z^2 - 2*z = 0
3*x^2*z + x^2 - 6*x*z - 2*x + 3*z = 0.
```

Elimination gives

```
27*z^4 - 121*z^3 + 179*z^2 - 96*z + 15 = 0
132*x = 27*z^4 - 526*z^3 + 941*z^2 - 87*z.
```

The relevant root is approximately `z=0.2654747088302300`,
`x=0.2539025418087003`. The checked-in rational approximation has exact same-period
regret below `1e-14`.

The follow-up implementation represents the selected root by its quartic and the open
rational isolating interval
`(26547470883022/10^14, 26547470883024/10^14)`. A Sturm sequence proves that the
interval contains exactly one root. The profile and every value/deviation are evaluated
in the rational polynomial quotient, with signs decided by gcd and interval refinement.
All eight player/start-phase regrets reduce exactly to zero. This is an exact
single-instance period-two certificate, still restricted to same-period deviations.
